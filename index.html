About

I am a Professor in the Department of International Economics and Law at Hankuk University of Foreign Studies. I received my B.A. in Economics from Seoul National University and my Ph.D. in Economics from Columbia University. My research lies at the intersection of industrial organization and international trade, with a focus on multinational firms and the dynamics of exporting firms. My research asks how firms’ international activities evolve over time and what drives these dynamics, drawing on firm-level panel data from Korea.
